# Performance-Based Apartment Design Guide — 6 Worked Site Exercises

Six different NSW apartment-site situations, each walked through the full workflow from the *369 Alliance Performance-Based Apartment Design Guide — General* (Parts 1–4 + NCC 2022 overlays).

Each exercise follows the same five-pass logic from Part 1:

1. **Site intelligence** (Part 1 + Part 3A)
2. **Massing strategy** (Part 2)
3. **Apartment performance** (Part 4 Amenity / Configuration)
4. **Regulated design** (NCC overlay, Type A/B/C, fire matrix)
5. **Occupation + operation** (Part 4 Performance — energy, water, waste, maintenance)

All addresses are illustrative/hypothetical for teaching. LEP/DCP figures shown are *typical for the locality* and must be re-verified against the current instrument before any real lodgement.

## Exercise 1 — Inner West Flat Infill (Marrickville)

### Address & site snapshot

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Address (illustrative)** | 14–18 Premier St, Marrickville NSW 2204 |
| **Council** | Inner West |
| **Zoning** | R3 Medium Density Residential (typical for the cluster) |
| **Site area** | 3 lots amalgamated, ~1,540 m² |
| **Frontage / depth** | 36 m / 43 m (rectangular, near-flat, 0.8 m fall front-to-rear) |
| **Aspect** | Long axis east–west; rear (south) faces lane |
| **Context** | 800 m walk to Marrickville Station; surrounded by single dwellings + 2-storey walk-ups |
| **Hazards** | Acid-sulfate Class 4; no flood, no heritage item on site |

### Part 1 — Identifying the context

- **1A Apartment building type:** 3-storey walk-up infill; *not* shop-top; sits in an established residential street.
- **1B Local character:** Federation cottages + scattered 1970s walk-ups. Street tree canopy is intermittent. Brick + tile dominant; low front fences; 3 m–6 m front gardens.
- **1C Precinct vs individual site:** Individual-site response. No precinct masterplan. Character review must address front-garden depth, front-fence type, and the brick palette.

### Part 2 — Developing the controls

| Control | Test | Outcome for this site |
|---|---|---|
| **2A Primary controls** | Read height, FSR, deep soil, basement, separation as one system | Anchor decision: keep height at 3 storeys with usable roof terrace screened to 12 m datum |
| **2B Building envelope** | Subtract 25–30 % for balconies, cores, articulation | Compliant envelope ~6,200 m³; achievable residential GFA ~1,680 m² |
| **2C Building height** | LEP 11 m / 3 storeys; **NCC effective height stays < 12 m** | Lift overrun + stair pressurisation to be screened *inside* 12 m envelope to keep DtS hydrant/sprinkler rules unchanged |
| **2D FSR** | LEP 1.1:1 = 1,694 m² GFA | Test 3 massing options: linear bar, rear-courtyard L, broken-down "two pavilions" — courtyard option chosen on amenity evidence |
| **2E Building depth** | Glass-line to glass-line | 12 m double-loaded south-aspect risk → use 11.4 m with dual-aspect corner units |
| **2F Building separation** | < 12 m height band | 6 m to non-habitable boundary, 9 m habitable-to-non-habitable, 12 m habitable-to-habitable across courtyard |
| **2G Street setback** | DCP 4.5 m garden setback | Hold 4.5 m, low brick fence, retain street tree |
| **2H Side / rear setbacks** | Side 3 m (habitable rooms), rear 6 m to lane | Reserve rear deep-soil zone & future-neighbour envelope test |
| **NCC Type** | Class 2, rise = 3 storeys → **Type C construction** | Lowest fire-resistance band; check Spec 5 for FRL on common walls / shafts |
| **Effective height** | < 12 m | Hydrants & sprinklers: *Check* (DtS likely not required); fire-isolated stairs: *Check*; FCR: *Not triggered* |

### Part 3 — Siting the development

- **3A Site analysis:** topo survey + tree register (2 x mature jacarandas to retain on rear boundary), stormwater to Premier St kerb, no easements, neighbouring window survey to north & south.
- **3B Orientation:** long axis east–west = good north exposure for living rooms; protect winter sun to courtyard 9:00–15:00 mid-winter.
- **3C Public domain interface:** front-loaded entry on Premier St with awning; low fence; planted privacy buffer to ground-floor windows.
- **3D Communal open space:** rear north-facing courtyard ~280 m² (≥ 25 % site area DtS), tested for mid-winter sun.
- **3E Deep soil:** rear 7 % strip + side margin = 9 % of site, sufficient for 2 retained + 3 new canopy trees; basement footprint pulled in 3 m from rear boundary.
- **3F Visual privacy:** 12 m / 9 m / 6 m matrix at < 12 m height band; angled bedroom windows on south façade to neighbour at #20.
- **3G Pedestrian access:** primary entry direct from footpath; accessible route 1:20 from boundary to lift; passive surveillance from L1 living room.
- **3H Vehicle access:** driveway off rear lane only — no driveway crossover on Premier St (preserves street tree + active edge).
- **3J Bicycle / car parking:** 0.5 spaces/unit (close to station) + secure bike room of 1 space/unit at lobby level; no EV-charging room required at this scale but conduits provided.

### Part 4 — Designing the building (key decisions)

- **4A Solar:** ≥ 70 % of apartments receive 2 hours mid-winter sun to principal living room — schedule by unit + 3D shadow study on neighbours at #12 and #20.
- **4B Cross ventilation:** 65 % of units genuinely cross-ventilated via courtyard + corner units; deep south-facing units excluded from count.
- **4C Ceiling heights:** 2.7 m habitable / 2.4 m wet; section coordination with services + sprinklers.
- **4D Apartment size & layout:** furnished plans for 1B, 2B, 2B+S, 3B; storage not borrowed from bedrooms.
- **4E Private open space:** balcony 8 m² (1B) / 10 m² (2B) / 12 m² (3B) with 2 m min depth; recessed where facing lane.
- **4G Storage:** schedule + tagged 3D location; 50 % inside apartment, 50 % in basement cages.
- **4H Acoustic privacy:** wet stacks aligned vertically; bedrooms not adjacent to lift shaft or garbage room.
- **4O Landscape:** retained jacarandas + new canopy + rain-garden along driveway edge.
- **4U Energy:** BASIX target met passively (orientation + façade depth); PV on flat roof.

### Performance Solutions flagged

- **None expected** at this scale on a flat infill site. DtS pathway viable across NCC + ADG provided 70 % solar + 60 % cross-ventilation are demonstrated.

### Approval evidence package

Site analysis, shadow study (mid-winter 9:00 / 12:00 / 15:00), apartment unit schedule with solar + ventilation tags, deep-soil plan, tree retention strategy, BASIX certificate, NCC Type C confirmation memo.

## Exercise 2 — Sloping Corner Site (Wollstonecraft, North Sydney)

### Address & site snapshot

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Address (illustrative)** | 3–7 Shirley Rd corner Romsey St, Wollstonecraft NSW 2065 |
| **Council** | North Sydney |
| **Zoning** | R4 High Density Residential |
| **Site area** | 1,820 m² (3 lots, corner) |
| **Slope** | 5.4 m fall from north-west (high) to south-east (low) — 1:8 gradient |
| **Aspect** | NE corner of intersection; long axis runs N–S |
| **Context** | Mix of 1960s walk-ups + recent 6–8 storey apartments; 600 m to Wollstonecraft Station |
| **Hazards** | Sandstone subgrade; no flood; bushfire BAL-LOW |

### Part 1 — Identifying the context

- **1A Type:** Mid-rise apartment (6–7 storeys) on a sloping corner — split into stepped massing.
- **1B Character:** Garden suburb scale transitioning to apartment renewal; sandstone retaining walls and mature trees on the slope are character defining.
- **1C Precinct/individual:** Corner site shapes both streets — must be read as a *precinct corner anchor* even though only one site.

### Part 2 — Developing the controls

| Control | Test | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **2A Primary controls** | Slope changes how height is read | Anchor: stepped 5–6–7 storey massing reading lower from upslope |
| **2B Envelope** | LEP plane + DCP street-wall + setbacks | Real envelope smaller than control prism due to street-wall step-back at Level 5 |
| **2C Height** | LEP 21 m. **Effective height ≈ 17.5 m** (between 12 m and 25 m) | Hydrants + sprinklers + fire-isolated stairs all *Required*; FCR *Check* |
| **2D FSR** | LEP 2.0:1 ≈ 3,640 m² GFA | Stepped-bar option vs courtyard option; courtyard chosen on solar evidence |
| **2E Depth** | Aim 12–14 m glass-to-glass | Acceptable; deep central units replaced with through-corner planning |
| **2F Separation** | 12–25 m band: 18 m habitable / 12 m mixed / 9 m non-hab | Internal courtyard 18 m wide |
| **2G Street setback** | DCP 5 m to Shirley Rd, 4 m to Romsey St | Garden setback retained; rebuild sandstone retaining wall |
| **2H Side / rear** | 6 m + step at upper levels | Future-neighbour envelope test critical on the south boundary |
| **NCC Type** | Class 2, rise = 7 storeys → **Type A construction** | Highest FRL band; check protected openings |
| **Effective height fire matrix** | 12–25 m band | Hydrants R, sprinklers R, fire-isolated stairs R, FCR Check, emergency lift —, smoke management Check |

### Part 3 — Siting the development

- **3A Site analysis:** detailed level survey on 250 mm grid; sandstone outcrop on NW corner triggers retention; tree report (2 x significant fig trees on Romsey St verge).
- **3B Orientation:** long axis N–S; west façade on Romsey St requires deep balconies + screening for summer heat.
- **3C Public domain:** corner address on Shirley/Romsey; lobby at the high point with accessible ramp 1:20 alongside steps; sandstone-toned podium.
- **3D Communal open space:** sky terrace at Level 5 step-back + ground-level garden on south side; usable dimension 6 m × 12 m minimum.
- **3E Deep soil:** ~15 % concentrated on south boundary (downslope) where soil profile is deepest.
- **3F Visual privacy:** sloping-site overlooking *checked in section* — upslope balconies look directly into downslope living rooms unless angled.
- **3G Pedestrian access:** continuous accessible route from Romsey St footpath to lobby despite 1.8 m level change.
- **3H Vehicle access:** basement entry from Romsey St (lower side) — minimises ramp grade and removes ramp from the primary Shirley Rd frontage.
- **3J Bicycle / car parking:** 1 sp/unit in 2-level basement; bike room at Romsey entry; EV charging room with ventilation + fire separation per NCC.

### Part 4 — Designing the building (key decisions)

- **4A Solar:** 70 % target met by stepping the south side of the courtyard down 1 storey to admit mid-winter sun.
- **4B Ventilation:** corner planning gives 60 % cross-ventilation; corridor ventilation via openable end windows on each level.
- **4C Ceilings:** 2.7 m typical; 3.0 m at top floor for cross ventilation + thermal stratification.
- **4D Size & layout:** mix targets 15 % studio / 40 % 1B / 35 % 2B / 10 % 3B; structural grid 8.4 m suits dual-aspect.
- **4F Common circulation:** double-loaded corridor capped at 15 m runs with a daylit window at each end.
- **4H Acoustic:** wet stacks vertically aligned; bedrooms not adjacent to lift or rubbish chute on any floor.
- **4M Façade:** depth + horizontal banding to break apparent height; sandstone-toned base ties to retained outcrop.
- **4N Roof:** PV array + plant screened *inside* 21 m envelope; communal roof terrace separated by acoustic + privacy screen from neighbour.
- **4Q Universal design:** ≥ 20 % adaptable units distributed across orientations and levels.

### Performance Solutions flagged

- **Effective height boundary:** if plant + lift overrun pushes the building over 25 m, the fire matrix moves into "emergency lift + FCR Required" band — a Performance Solution may justify keeping a single fire-isolated stair if the fire engineer can prove equivalent egress; otherwise design around the limit.
- **Sloping-site separation in section:** privacy is solved with angled bedroom windows on the upslope side — Performance Solution memo with sightline diagrams.

### Approval evidence package

Site survey + sandstone outcrop plan, stepped-massing 3D, future-neighbour envelope test on south boundary, slope-section privacy diagrams, NCC Type A FRL schedule, fire engineering brief, BASIX, landscape & deep-soil plan, basement EV ventilation strategy.

## Exercise 3 — Heritage-Adjacent Infill (Glebe)

### Address & site snapshot

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Address (illustrative)** | 22–26 Mitchell St, Glebe NSW 2037 |
| **Council** | City of Sydney |
| **Zoning** | R1 General Residential |
| **Site area** | 1,150 m² |
| **Heritage** | Site is *within* a Heritage Conservation Area; #20 next door is a listed Victorian terrace |
| **Frontage** | 21 m, near-flat with 0.6 m crossfall |
| **Context** | Continuous Victorian/Edwardian terrace street, 2-storey scale |
| **Hazards** | None significant; check archaeological potential for early colonial fabric |

### Part 1 — Identifying the context

- **1A Type:** 3-storey + roof-form apartment building — must read as terrace-scale infill.
- **1B Character:** Victorian terrace street with parapet rhythm, 5.5 m party-wall spacing, recessed front gardens, sandstone kerbs. Character is *the* primary control.
- **1C Precinct:** Strong precinct identity — the conservation-area DCP overrides generic apartment massing.

### Part 2 — Developing the controls

| Control | Test | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **2A** | Heritage controls displace some ADG matters via Housing SEPP s.149 | Heritage CMP + DCP control rule the form |
| **2B Envelope** | Capped by HCA streetwall + party-wall pattern | Real envelope much smaller than R1 prism would suggest |
| **2C Height** | LEP 11 m / 3 storeys, *but* DCP eaves line ~ 6.5 m to match terrace parapets | 3rd storey set back min 4 m from front wall as recessed attic |
| **2D FSR** | LEP 0.9:1 ≈ 1,035 m² | ~10 units; cannot exceed without losing character outcome |
| **2E Depth** | 11 m glass-to-glass to allow rear courtyard | Single rear-court apartment per floor |
| **2F Separation** | < 12 m band: 12/9/6 | Met internally; party-wall to listed #20 needs noise + structural memo |
| **2G Street setback** | Match terrace front-garden line | Inherit existing 3 m setback exactly |
| **2H Side/rear** | 0 m party walls (terrace context); 6 m rear | Continuous party walls + rear deep-soil garden |
| **NCC Type** | Class 2, rise 3 storeys → **Type C** | Type C with Spec 5 FRL on retained party walls |
| **Effective height** | < 12 m | DtS fire systems mostly *Check* level |

### Part 3 — Siting the development

- **3A Site analysis:** heritage CMP, archaeological assessment, party-wall condition survey on #20 + #28, street tree register, kerb + paving heritage.
- **3B Orientation:** long axis E–W; rear garden faces south — unfavourable but typical. Living rooms placed on north (front) side; bedrooms rear.
- **3C Public domain:** front fence type drawn from precinct DCP (cast iron palisade + sandstone plinth); paint colours from CMP heritage palette.
- **3D Communal open space:** rear-garden communal space ~ 25 % of site; level entry from ground-floor lift lobby.
- **3E Deep soil:** rear 35 % uninterrupted by basement; tank + plant relocated into roof void.
- **3F Visual privacy:** habitable rooms avoid direct overlooking into #20 listed terrace's first-floor windows; sill heights raised on side facade or angled offsets.
- **3G Pedestrian access:** single front entry through restored carriage opening; level threshold + accessible ramp behind front fence.
- **3H Vehicle access:** **no basement** — unviable due to heritage subgrade + small footprint. On-street + car-share only (City of Sydney precedent).
- **3J Bicycle parking:** generous bike room at lobby (1.5 sp/unit) compensates for zero parking; pram + mobility storage at ground level.

### Part 4 — Designing the building (key decisions)

- **4A Solar:** because of E–W long axis on a south-rear lot, only ~50 % of units get 2-hour mid-winter sun to living rooms — *Performance Solution* required showing best-practicable outcome.
- **4B Ventilation:** dual-aspect (front + rear) on each unit gives 100 % cross ventilation — strong response.
- **4C Ceilings:** 3.0 m on ground floor (matches Victorian terrace datum); 2.7 m above.
- **4D Size & layout:** boutique mix — 6 x 2B + 4 x 3B family units to suit local demographic.
- **4E Private open space:** Juliet balconies on front for heritage compliance; rear balconies generous + planted.
- **4M Façade:** masonry + render + sandstone base reading as *one terrace*, not an apartment block; recessed top floor invisible from footpath sightline.
- **4O Landscape:** restored rear garden with retained mature tree; deep-soil planted with locally indigenous species.
- **4R Adaptive reuse:** if any retained fabric (e.g. existing rear addition), document existing condition + upgrade section.
- **4U Energy:** BASIX achieved via envelope quality + roof PV; no west-facing glazing.

### Performance Solutions flagged

- **4A Solar < 70 %:** Performance Solution with best-practicable evidence (alternative orientation impossible without breaching HCA controls); compensated by superior cross ventilation + outlook + heritage outcome.
- **3J Zero on-site parking:** within City of Sydney precedent for HCA infill near transport — needs Council parking-strategy memo.

### Approval evidence package

Heritage Impact Statement, archaeological assessment, party-wall condition + structural protection plan, CMP-aligned material schedule, sightline analysis to listed #20, shadow study + best-practicable solar memo, BASIX, NCC Type C FRL schedule, parking-strategy report.

## Exercise 4 — Large Amalgamated Renewal Site (Macquarie Park, Ryde)

### Address & site snapshot

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Address (illustrative)** | 100–120 Talavera Rd, Macquarie Park NSW 2113 |
| **Council** | City of Ryde |
| **Zoning** | MU1 Mixed Use (renewal precinct) |
| **Site area** | 8,400 m² (6 lots amalgamated) |
| **Frontage** | 95 m to Talavera Rd, secondary frontage to a planned new through-block lane |
| **Context** | Macquarie Park renewal — towers 12–28 storeys nearby, Metro station 350 m |
| **Hazards** | Some site contamination from former commercial use; remediation report required |

### Part 1 — Identifying the context

- **1A Type:** Multi-tower podium development — 2 residential towers above a 4-storey podium with retail + childcare at ground.
- **1B Character:** Emerging high-rise renewal precinct; character is *new* and being formed by this generation of buildings. Public-domain rules dominate (street tree, awning, podium height).
- **1C Precinct:** This is *the* precinct response — large enough to *create* the public realm: through-site link, public plaza, green spine.

### Part 2 — Developing the controls

| Control | Test | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **2A** | Major-project coordination — multiple controls + a precinct masterplan | Anchor: 2 slender towers (max plate ~750 m²) over 4-storey podium |
| **2B Envelope** | LEP gives prismatic envelope; DCP requires podium + tower separation | Real envelope = podium 4 storeys (~16 m) + 2 towers above (24 storeys / ~78 m) |
| **2C Height** | LEP 80 m | **Effective height ≈ 75 m → fire matrix top band** |
| **2D FSR** | LEP 4.5:1 ≈ 37,800 m² GFA | Distribution across podium retail (~3,500 m²), childcare (800 m²), ~33,500 m² residential |
| **2E Depth** | Tower plate 18 m max → centre core + dual-aspect units | Depth controlled; avoid single-aspect deep apartments |
| **2F Separation** | > 25 m band: 24 m habitable, 18 m mixed, 12 m non-hab | Tower-to-tower 24 m centreline minimum |
| **2G Street setback** | Podium to street-line with awning; tower set back 6 m from podium edge | Active edge + reduced apparent bulk |
| **2H Side/rear** | 6 m podium side; tower step-back 6 m | Future-neighbour test critical — both adjoining sites are likely to redevelop |
| **NCC Type** | Class 2 + 6 + 9b (childcare), rise 28 storeys → **Type A** | High FRL throughout; mixed-class construction Part A6 logic |
| **Effective height fire matrix** | > 50 m | All systems *Required*: hydrant, sprinkler, FCR, emergency lift, smoke mgmt, fire-isolated stairs |

### Part 3 — Siting the development

- **3A Site analysis:** contamination delineation + remediation plan, geotechnical (deep basement implications), wind study, shadow study on adjoining residential to the south, traffic study.
- **3B Orientation:** long site axis NE–SW; orient towers to maximise N exposure; protect Talavera Rd footpath from down-wash with podium awning + tower setback.
- **3C Public domain:** create a **through-site link** with retail spine, plaza outside childcare, generous footpath widening on Talavera Rd.
- **3D Communal open space:** podium roof at L4 = communal landscaped terrace ~1,500 m² with shade, BBQ, play, with mid-winter sun tested.
- **3E Deep soil:** challenging on a fully built-out podium — concentrate ≥ 7 % deep soil at ground in the through-site link + rear tree pockets; podium roof = engineered planting (Part 4P).
- **3F Visual privacy:** > 25 m height band → 24 m tower-to-tower minimum; sky-view test from neighbouring residential.
- **3G Pedestrian access:** multiple lobbies (one per tower + childcare entry + retail); separate residential lobby from retail loading.
- **3H Vehicle access:** loading dock + waste collection from rear secondary lane; resident basement access from a side street; childcare drop-off = signed kerbside zone.
- **3J Bicycle / car parking:** 3-level basement; 1 sp/unit residential, retail per DCP, end-of-trip facilities for retail staff, abundant bike storage near each lobby.

### Part 4 — Designing the building (key decisions)

- **4A Solar:** apartment-by-apartment shadow study; ≥ 70 % living rooms with 2-hr mid-winter sun; *neighbour* shadow impact on existing residential to south is the political/planning risk.
- **4B Ventilation:** centre-core tower with 8 units/floor → 6 corner + 2 deep-end; cross ventilation 75 % via through + corner units.
- **4C Ceilings:** 2.7 m typical; 3.3 m podium retail.
- **4D Size & layout:** podium townhouse-style ground apartments; tower mix 10 % studio / 40 % 1B / 35 % 2B / 15 % 3B.
- **4F Common circulation:** short corridors from centre core; daylit lift lobbies via single-loaded leg.
- **4H Acoustic:** structural break between residential tower and childcare/retail podium; floating floor over plant zones.
- **4J Noise & pollution:** Talavera Rd traffic noise — acoustic façade + winter-garden balconies on the road side.
- **4K Mix:** mixed across both towers; do not cluster all 3B family units in one tower.
- **4M Façade:** modulated tower façade + warm masonry podium; long-life materials; cleaning & replacement strategy designed in.
- **4N Roof:** PV + plant + access; communal terrace below.
- **4O Landscape + 4P Planting on structures:** podium-roof communal landscape engineered with structural engineer + landscape architect — soil depth, drainage cells, root barriers, irrigation.
- **4S Mixed use:** retail + childcare separated from residential entries and bedrooms; loading + waste collected away from residential lobby.
- **4U Energy:** electrified, all-electric services + PV + embedded network; meets BASIX + Net Zero precinct target.
- **4V Water:** rainwater harvesting, WSUD across plaza + through-link, OSD tank in basement.
- **4W Waste:** chute + tri-stream sorting room per tower; bulky-waste room; vehicle swept-path at loading dock.
- **4X Maintenance:** BMU on tower roof, anchor points, façade access strategy documented at DA.

### Performance Solutions flagged

- **Acoustic façade vs cross ventilation (Talavera Rd):** balcony wintergardens with operable acoustic doors — Performance Solution proving acceptable indoor noise + thermal comfort.
- **Podium-roof deep-soil substitute:** Performance Solution showing engineered planting equivalent canopy + cooling outcome.
- **Mixed-class fire separation (childcare + residential + retail):** Performance Solution coordinated with fire engineer covering compartmentation + egress.

### Approval evidence package

Masterplan + DA, contamination + remediation, geotechnical, wind tunnel report, traffic + parking, shadow studies on neighbours, NCC Type A schedule + fire engineering brief, BASIX + Net Zero pathway, podium landscape engineering set, BMU + maintenance access plan, RAB Act regulated design declarations.

## Exercise 5 — Noisy Frontage (Strathfield, Parramatta Rd)

### Address & site snapshot

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Address (illustrative)** | 245–251 Parramatta Rd, Strathfield NSW 2135 |
| **Council** | Strathfield |
| **Zoning** | E2 Commercial Centre / R4 transition (typical for Parramatta Rd corridor) |
| **Site area** | 2,200 m² |
| **Frontage** | 38 m to Parramatta Rd (heavy traffic, 24/7 noise, freight) |
| **Slope** | Flat |
| **Context** | Mixed shop-top + small lots; pedestrian environment poor; rear lane access |
| **Hazards** | Severe road-traffic noise + air-quality risk; potential historic contamination |

### Part 1 — Identifying the context

- **1A Type:** 6-storey shop-top mixed use — retail at ground, apartments above.
- **1B Character:** Parramatta Rd corridor — auto-oriented, fragmented frontages. Character is being *replaced* by renewal.
- **1C Precinct:** Individual-site response with corridor logic; align building line + awning continuity to emerging street wall.

### Part 2 — Developing the controls

| Control | Test | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **2A** | Noise dictates plan + façade form before envelope | Anchor: courtyard plan with bedrooms turned *inward* |
| **2B Envelope** | LEP + corridor street-wall control | 4-storey street wall + 2 storey set back 5 m |
| **2C Height** | LEP 21 m. **Effective height ~18 m → 12–25 m band** | Hydrants R, sprinklers R, fire-isolated stairs R |
| **2D FSR** | LEP 2.5:1 ≈ 5,500 m² | Retail 600 m² + residential ~4,900 m² |
| **2E Depth** | 14 m glass-to-glass; with central courtyard plate is workable | Avoid single-aspect deep south-facing units |
| **2F Separation** | Internal courtyard ≥ 12 m | DtS met |
| **2G Street setback** | Zero to Parramatta Rd (active commercial edge) | Awning + recessed residential entry |
| **2H Side/rear** | 6 m rear to lane; future-envelope test on flank | Reserve future-neighbour lit interface |
| **NCC Type** | Class 2 + 6, rise 6 storeys → **Type A** | Highest FRL; mixed-use Part A6 |
| **Effective height fire matrix** | 12–25 m band | Hydrants R, sprinklers R, fire-isolated stairs R, FCR Check, emergency lift —, smoke mgmt Check |

### Part 3 — Siting the development

- **3A Site analysis:** acoustic survey (24-hour LAeq + Lmax), air-quality / NOx, contamination assessment, neighbouring window & private-open-space survey.
- **3B Orientation:** front (Parramatta Rd) faces north — solar opportunity *but* noise + pollution risk; resolved with wintergarden on north façade.
- **3C Public domain:** continuous awning, set-back residential entry from retail address, planted buffer between footpath and podium glazing.
- **3D Communal open space:** rear courtyard (south-of-building) sheltered from road noise + on Level 4 podium roof terrace away from road.
- **3E Deep soil:** small ground-deep-soil pocket at rear; podium-roof landscape compensates (engineered planting per 4P).
- **3F Visual privacy:** rear apartments overlook neighbour rear yards — angle sightlines + planting.
- **3G Pedestrian access:** clearly separate residential entry from retail; awning sheltered; lobby fully internal away from road noise.
- **3H Vehicle access:** rear lane only — *no* driveway crossover on Parramatta Rd.
- **3J Bicycle / car parking:** 0.7 sp/unit + secure bike room near rear lobby; loading dock for retail off rear lane.

### Part 4 — Designing the building (key decisions)

- **4A Solar:** north-facing units have solar opportunity *but* noise complicates open-window living; recess balconies + winter-garden detail.
- **4B Cross ventilation:** **CANNOT count a unit cross-ventilated if the road-facing window must stay closed for noise** — count only courtyard-aspect or dual-aspect rear units. Realistic cross-ventilation count ~40 %.
- **4C Ceilings:** 2.7 m typical; 3.3 m retail.
- **4D Size & layout:** mix favours 1B + 2B; bedrooms turned to courtyard or rear; living rooms can address Parramatta Rd via wintergarden.
- **4E Private open space:** wintergardens on Parramatta Rd front (acoustic glazing + operable inner panels); open balconies on courtyard + rear.
- **4H Acoustic privacy:** internal — wet stacks aligned, bedrooms not adjacent to lift, full-height acoustic separation between retail and residential L1 floor.
- **4J Noise & pollution (PRIMARY):** acoustic façade designed to AS/NZS 2107 internal noise targets; mechanical ventilation with filtered fresh-air supply when windows must stay closed; balconies designed as *acoustic refuges* (recessed, screened).
- **4M Façade:** street-side façade engineered for acoustic + thermal performance; depth + screens; internal courtyard façade lighter, more open.
- **4S Mixed use:** retail + residential separated by floor + acoustic + entry; no late-trading uses adjoining residential.
- **4U Energy:** mechanical ventilation + heat recovery (since natural ventilation constrained); BASIX achieved with envelope + services strategy.

### Performance Solutions flagged

- **4B Cross ventilation < 60 % DtS:** Performance Solution backed by acoustic + air-quality reports demonstrating the road-facing units cannot rely on operable windows; alternative pathway = mechanical fresh-air + best-practicable orientation.

### Five-pass workflow applied to all six

1. **Site intelligence** — survey, neighbours, hazards, heritage, contamination, acoustic + air-quality, future-envelope test on every boundary.
2. **Massing strategy** — controls tested as one system (height + FSR + depth + separation + setbacks + deep soil + basement) before any preferred envelope is fixed.
3. **Apartment performance** — solar / ventilation / depth / privacy / circulation tested apartment-by-apartment with furniture overlays + 3D shadow.
4. **Regulated design** — NCC class + rise + Type A/B/C + effective-height fire matrix + DBP/RAB declarations coordinated with the architecture before lodgement.
5. **Occupation + operation** — energy, water, waste, maintenance and the BMU/access strategy designed in at DA stage so the approved performance survives construction.

### Universal rule from Part 1

> *Do not advance a design simply because a drawing fits the control envelope. Advance it when the evidence shows the apartment can perform across planning quality, NCC safety, sustainability, construction coordination and operation.*
