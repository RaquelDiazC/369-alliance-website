## Slide 3

SECURITY
Cash Retention: money held by the employer/head contractor as a safeguard for any defective work or late completion work by the contractor.
Retention is set at 3% or 5% of the total work value.
It can be deducted from payments made to the contractor, usually 10% per month until achieve the amount of 3% or 5%.
It is realised 50% of the total (1.5% or 2.5%) on Practical Completion (Date of PC) and another 50% at the End of Defects Period.
