Inner West Flat Infill

14–18 Premier St, Marrickville NSW 2204 · Inner West Council
R3 Medium Density 3 lots · ~1,540 m² Near-flat (0.8 m fall) 800 m to Marrickville Stn
Frontage / depth
36 m / 43 m, long axis E–W
Context
Federation cottages + 1970s walk-ups
Hazards
Acid-sulfate Class 4; no flood; no heritage

P1Identifying the context

  • 1A Type: 3-storey walk-up infill
  • 1B Character: brick + tile, intermittent canopy, 3–6 m gardens, low fences
  • 1C Precinct: individual-site response — character review on garden depth, fence type, brick palette

P2Developing the controls

2A primary3-storey + screened roof terrace inside 12 m datum
2B envelope~6,200 m³ container · ~1,680 m² achievable GFA
2C heightLEP 11 m / 3 storeys; effective height < 12 m
2D FSR1.1:1 ≈ 1,694 m² · courtyard option chosen on amenity
2E depth11.4 m glass-to-glass, dual-aspect corners
2F separation12 / 9 / 6 m at < 12 m band
2G street4.5 m garden setback, low brick fence, retain street tree
2H side/rear3 m sides · 6 m rear lane · deep-soil reserve
NCC typeType C
Effective height< 12 m

P3Siting

  • 3A: tree register (2 jacarandas retained) + window survey
  • 3B: long axis E–W → north exposure for living rooms; courtyard sun 9–15 mid-winter
  • 3C: front-loaded entry on Premier St + planted privacy buffer
  • 3D: rear north-facing courtyard ~280 m² (≥ 25 % site)
  • 3E: ~9 % deep soil; basement pulled 3 m off rear boundary
  • 3F: angled bedroom windows on south façade to #20
  • 3G: 1:20 accessible route boundary to lift
  • 3H: driveway off rear lane only — no street crossover
  • 3J: 0.5 sp/unit + 1 bike sp/unit · EV conduit only

P4Designing the building

  • 4A: ≥ 70 % units 2 hr mid-winter sun · neighbour shadow study
  • 4B: 65 % cross-vent via courtyard + corner units
  • 4C/D/E: 2.7 m ceilings · furnished 1B/2B/2B+S/3B · 8–12 m² balconies
  • 4G/H: tagged storage · wet stacks aligned · bedrooms off lift & chute
  • 4O/U: retained jacarandas + new canopy · roof PV · BASIX passive

PSPerformance Solutions / Evidence

Performance Solutions: none expected — DtS pathway viable.

Evidence pack: site analysis · shadow study (9/12/15 mid-winter) · unit schedule with solar+vent tags · deep-soil plan · tree retention · BASIX · NCC Type C confirmation memo.

Sloping Corner Mid-Rise

3–7 Shirley Rd cnr Romsey St, Wollstonecraft NSW 2065 · North Sydney
R4 High Density 3 lots · 1,820 m² 5.4 m fall · 1:8 600 m to station
Slope
NW (high) → SE (low) — stepped massing
Context
1960s walk-ups + new 6–8 storey apartments
Hazards
Sandstone subgrade · BAL-LOW · no flood

P1Identifying the context

  • 1A Type: 6–7 storey stepped mid-rise, lower from upslope
  • 1B Character: garden suburb → apartment renewal · sandstone walls + mature trees character-defining
  • 1C Precinct: read as a precinct corner anchor even on a single site

P2Developing the controls

2A primaryStepped 5–6–7 storey reading lower upslope
2B envelopeReal envelope < control prism (street-wall step at L5)
2C heightLEP 21 m · effective height ~17.5 m
2D FSR2.0:1 ≈ 3,640 m² · courtyard option (solar evidence)
2E depth12–14 m · through-corner planning replaces deep units
2F separation12–25 m band: 18 / 12 / 9 m
2G street5 m Shirley · 4 m Romsey · sandstone retaining rebuild
2H side/rear6 m + upper-level step · future-neighbour test critical
NCC typeType A
Effective height12–25 m band

P3Siting

  • 3A: 250 mm grid level survey · sandstone outcrop NW retained · 2 fig trees on Romsey verge
  • 3B: long axis N–S; west façade balcony depth + screening
  • 3C: corner lobby at high point · 1:20 accessible ramp + steps
  • 3D: sky terrace at L5 step-back + south ground garden
  • 3E: ~15 % deep soil concentrated downslope (south)
  • 3F: sloping-site overlooking checked in section
  • 3G: continuous accessible route from Romsey footpath
  • 3H: basement entry from Romsey (lower) — minimises ramp
  • 3J: 1 sp/unit · 2-level basement · EV room ventilation + FRL

P4Designing the building

  • 4A: 70 % solar by stepping south side of courtyard down 1 storey
  • 4B/F: 60 % cross-vent · corridors capped 15 m, daylit ends
  • 4C/D: 2.7 m typical · 3.0 m top floor · 8.4 m grid · 15/40/35/10 % mix
  • 4H: wet stacks aligned · bedrooms off lift & chute
  • 4M/N/Q: sandstone-toned base · screened plant + PV · ≥ 20 % adaptable

PSPerformance Solutions / Evidence

  • Effective height limit: if plant pushes > 25 m → emergency lift + FCR Required (PS or design-around)
  • Sloping-site privacy: angled upslope bedroom windows + sightline diagrams

Evidence pack: survey + sandstone outcrop · stepped-massing 3D · future-neighbour envelope (south) · slope-section privacy · NCC Type A FRL schedule · fire-engineering brief · BASIX · landscape + deep-soil · EV ventilation.

Heritage-Adjacent Infill

22–26 Mitchell St, Glebe NSW 2037 · City of Sydney
R1 General Residential 1,150 m² Heritage Conservation Area Listed terrace at #20
Frontage
21 m, near-flat with 0.6 m crossfall
Context
Continuous Victorian / Edwardian terrace street
Hazards
Archaeological potential; party-wall risk

P1Identifying the context

  • 1A Type: 3-storey + recessed roof-form terrace-scale infill
  • 1B Character: Victorian parapet rhythm, 5.5 m party-wall cadence, sandstone kerbs — character is the primary control
  • 1C Precinct: HCA DCP overrides generic apartment massing (Housing SEPP s.149 displacement)

P2Developing the controls

2A primaryHeritage CMP + DCP rule the form, not LEP prism
2B envelopeReal envelope much smaller than R1 prism
2C heightLEP 11 m · DCP eaves ~6.5 m · 3rd storey set back ≥ 4 m
2D FSR0.9:1 ≈ 1,035 m² · ~10 boutique units
2E depth11 m glass-to-glass · single rear-court apartment / floor
2F separation< 12 m band 12/9/6 met internally
2G streetInherit 3 m terrace setback exactly · cast-iron palisade
2H side/rear0 m party walls · 6 m rear deep-soil garden
NCC typeType C
Effective height< 12 m

P3Siting

  • 3A: heritage CMP · archaeological assessment · party-wall survey #20 + #28
  • 3B: long axis E–W · south-rear unfavourable · living rooms north
  • 3C: restored carriage opening as front entry · CMP palette
  • 3D: rear-garden communal space ~25 % of site, level entry
  • 3E: 35 % rear deep soil · tank + plant relocated to roof void
  • 3F: raised sills / offset windows protect #20 first-floor windows
  • 3G: level threshold + accessible ramp behind front fence
  • 3H: NO basement (heritage subgrade + small footprint)
  • 3J: 1.5 bike sp/unit + pram store · zero on-site car parking

P4Designing the building

  • 4A: ~50 % solar — best-practicable evidence required
  • 4B: dual-aspect every unit → 100 % cross-vent (strong response)
  • 4C/D/E: 3.0 m ground · 2.7 m above · 6×2B + 4×3B · Juliet front, generous rear balconies
  • 4M/O: reads as one terrace · restored rear garden + retained tree
  • 4R/U: existing-fabric upgrade documented · BASIX via envelope + roof PV

PSPerformance Solutions / Evidence

  • Solar < 70 % (4A): best-practicable PS · compensated by cross-vent + outlook + heritage outcome
  • Zero on-site parking (3J): Council parking-strategy memo (HCA + transport precedent)

Evidence pack: Heritage Impact Statement · archaeology · party-wall protection · CMP material schedule · sightline analysis to #20 · best-practicable solar memo · BASIX · NCC Type C FRL · parking strategy.

Amalgamated Renewal Site

100–120 Talavera Rd, Macquarie Park NSW 2113 · City of Ryde
MU1 Mixed Use 6 lots · 8,400 m² Towers 12–28 storeys nearby 350 m to Metro
Frontage
95 m + new through-block lane
Context
Renewal precinct — public realm being formed by this generation
Hazards
Site contamination (former commercial) — remediation required

P1Identifying the context

  • 1A Type: 2 slender residential towers above a 4-storey podium with retail + childcare
  • 1B Character: emerging high-rise renewal — public-domain rules dominate
  • 1C Precinct: large enough to create the public realm — through-site link, plaza, green spine

P2Developing the controls

2A primary2 towers (max plate ~750 m²) + 4-storey podium
2B envelopePodium 16 m + towers ~78 m, 6 m tower setback off podium edge
2C heightLEP 80 m · effective height ~75 m → top-band fire matrix
2D FSR4.5:1 ≈ 37,800 m² · retail 3,500 + childcare 800 + res ~33,500
2E depthTower plate 18 m max · centre core · dual-aspect units
2F separation> 25 m band: 24 / 18 / 12 m · tower-to-tower 24 m
2G streetPodium to street + awning · towers set back 6 m
2H side/rear6 m podium · tower step-back 6 m · future-neighbour critical
NCC typeType A · Class 2+6+9b
Effective height> 50 m — all systems Required

P3Siting

  • 3A: contamination delineation · geotech · wind study · neighbour shadow · traffic
  • 3B: long axis NE–SW · maximise N · podium awning + tower setback for footpath comfort
  • 3C: through-site link · retail spine · plaza outside childcare
  • 3D: L4 podium-roof terrace ~1,500 m², shaded + sunny zones
  • 3E: ≥ 7 % deep soil concentrated at ground link + tree pockets
  • 3F: 24 m tower-to-tower + sky-view test from neighbours
  • 3G: separate residential lobbies / childcare / retail
  • 3H: loading + waste from rear lane · resident basement off side street
  • 3J: 3-level basement · 1 sp/unit · end-of-trip retail · bike near each lobby

P4Designing the building

  • 4A/B: ≥ 70 % solar by-unit · 75 % cross-vent (corner + through)
  • 4C/D: 2.7 m res · 3.3 m retail · 10/40/35/15 mix · podium townhouse units
  • 4H/J: structural break res ↔ podium · acoustic façade to Talavera · winter-gardens road side
  • 4O/P: engineered podium-roof landscape · soil depth + drainage cells + irrigation
  • 4S/U/V/W/X: all-electric + PV + embedded network · WSUD · tri-stream waste · BMU + façade access

PSPerformance Solutions / Evidence

  • Acoustic façade vs cross-vent: wintergardens with operable acoustic doors
  • Podium-roof deep-soil substitute: equivalent canopy + cooling outcome
  • Mixed-class fire separation: childcare + residential + retail compartmentation + egress

Evidence pack: masterplan · contamination + remediation · geotech · wind tunnel · traffic · neighbour shadow · NCC Type A schedule · fire engineering · BASIX + Net Zero · podium landscape engineering · BMU · RAB Act regulated declarations.

Noisy Frontage Shop-Top

245–251 Parramatta Rd, Strathfield NSW 2135 · Strathfield Council
E2 Commercial Centre 2,200 m² 24/7 traffic + freight noise Air-quality risk
Frontage
38 m to Parramatta Rd · rear lane access
Context
Mixed shop-top, fragmented frontages, weak pedestrian environment
Hazards
Severe road noise + air quality · potential historic contamination

P1Identifying the context

  • 1A Type: 6-storey shop-top mixed use — retail at ground, apartments above
  • 1B Character: Parramatta Rd corridor, auto-oriented, character being replaced by renewal
  • 1C Precinct: individual-site with corridor logic — align street wall + awning continuity

P2Developing the controls

2A primaryNoise dictates plan + façade — courtyard plan with bedrooms inward
2B envelope4-storey street wall + 2-storey set back 5 m
2C heightLEP 21 m · effective height ~18 m → 12–25 m band
2D FSR2.5:1 ≈ 5,500 m² · retail 600 + res ~4,900 m²
2E depth14 m glass-to-glass · central courtyard plate workable
2F separationInternal courtyard ≥ 12 m
2G streetZero to Parramatta Rd · awning + recessed res entry
2H side/rear6 m rear · future-envelope test on flank
NCC typeType A · Class 2+6
Effective height12–25 m band

P3Siting

  • 3A: 24-hr acoustic survey · NOx air quality · contamination · neighbour windows + POS
  • 3B: front faces north — solar opportunity but noise risk → wintergarden response
  • 3C: continuous awning · set-back residential entry · planted buffer to glazing
  • 3D: rear courtyard sheltered from road + L4 podium roof terrace
  • 3E: small ground deep-soil + engineered podium-roof landscape (4P)
  • 3F: rear units overlook neighbour yards — angled sightlines + planting
  • 3G: separate residential entry from retail · lobby fully internal
  • 3H: rear lane only — NO Parramatta Rd crossover
  • 3J: 0.7 sp/unit · bike room near rear lobby · retail loading off lane

P4Designing the building

  • 4A: N solar opportunity but open-window living constrained
  • 4B: CANNOT count road-facing units cross-vented · realistic ~40 %
  • 4D/E: bedrooms turned to courtyard / rear · wintergardens road side
  • 4J: acoustic façade to AS/NZS 2107 · filtered mechanical fresh-air · acoustic refuges
  • 4S/U: retail / residential separated by floor + acoustic + entry · mech vent + heat recovery

PSPerformance Solutions / Evidence

  • Cross-vent < 60 % (4B): acoustic + air-quality reports + mechanical fresh-air alternative
  • Acoustic façade DtS (4J): internal noise targets achieved + maintained
  • Communal sun (3D): podium-roof terrace as compensating amenity

Evidence pack: 24-hr acoustic + air quality · acoustic façade spec · mech ventilation strategy · contamination + remediation · BASIX · NCC Type A FRL + Part A6 mixed-class · fire engineering · evidenced cross-vent count · neighbour-impact privacy.
